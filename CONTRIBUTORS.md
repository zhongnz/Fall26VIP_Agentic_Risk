## Where contribution evidence lives

- **Throughout the project:** each student's Individual tasks link their work,
  PRs, substantive reviews, and artifacts, including research and useful failed attempts.
- **At the report checkpoints:** each student writes an [individual contribution
  report](docs/contributors/README.md#individual-contribution-report) explaining
  their decisions and learning with links to that evidence.
- **At handoff:** each pair report includes a short, reviewed summary
  of who did what. Potential paper authorship is a separate documented decision.

There is no separate portfolio logging requirement. Preserve any existing records
as evidence; new students need no personal profile file or contribution table.
The founding scaffold and scripted pilot predate student enrollment: credit later
reproduction, critique, and improvements accurately. Activity counts do not measure
contribution quality.
